I wanted to create an overview page to remind myself and anyone else who may want to contribute what consititutes a "hunt" on this page. In an enterprise environment I would expect one hunt to contain multiple queries (4-6), where each query maps to a Mitre TTP. The goal being for these queries to identify the kill chain a specific Threat Actor, Malware, or group utilizes.

That being said, since this site is attempting to be relatively agnostic and act as a knowledge base we will aim to keep our hunts at 1-3 queries that will try to identify a single behavior or technique. The one exception to this rule will be infrastructure hunts/profiles that may require additional research pivoting to make sense of the data.

As to our methodology we will follow a PEAK methodology ([[Peak Threat Hunting]]) for our hunt structure in general. If we find a methodology that we like more in the future or find another methodology that makes more sense for a particular hunt we will note at the top of the article and a corresponding overview article will be made in the "Hunting Theory" section.
